# FEATURES.md — what the game contains

The game as it stands: **every feature below works and was verified in the browser**. This file describes the features; the checklist of what is done and the place for the next ideas live in [PLAN.md](PLAN.md).

Status legend: **done** — implemented and working.

## Core gameplay

| Feature | Status | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Walking (← / →) with collision | done | `src/domain/actors/player.js` against the solids of the section (the walls that close the ends of the street). Blocked → Rudy keeps walking in place. Side arrows stay live during a jump, so he can drift mid-hop |
| Punching (Space) | done | **Every press is a punch** (owner 2026-10-12): no player delay (`recoveryMs: 0`), presses made during a blow queue up and fire right after it. The hands alternate every other punch (frames 0–3 / 4–7, the move's `alternate` flag). The blow resolves at full extension — 1 damage, hit/miss sounds. Reach is measured **hitbox-edge to hitbox-edge** over the narrow hitboxes of the character configs (`w 20` for Rudy and `lysy-blokers`, `w 40` for `zul-mietek`) |
| Combat: first to strike + timed evasion | done | Damage lands at full extension; the blows are arbitrated in finish-time order, not in update order. A landed blow interrupts the defender. The **jump** cancels a windup immediately and dodges melee and vomit; a late dodge cannot undo damage already taken. Player recovery stays zero |
| RPS tie-break on simultaneous strikes | done | Already-started melee attacks finishing within **100 ms inclusive** use headbutt > punch > kick > headbutt, without delaying a single strike. The same move type favors the earlier finish; identical finish times draw (both miss). The boss's `vomit` never takes part |
| Move sets per character (key + sprite row) | done | Configured per character in `data/characters/*.json` — `moves: [{ key, row, type, alternate }]`, never hardcoded. `lysy-blokers` has a headbutt only, Rudy a punch, `zul-mietek` a spit |
| Character stats via config files | done | `data/characters/*.json`: hp, walk and attack speed, the hold, the recovery, the hitbox, the sprite name, the fall row and the AI `behavior` (`patrol` / `sentry`). The ranged attack is **derived**: a character with a `vomit` move gets `attackKind: "ranged"` |
| Jump (↑, walk cycle in the air) | done | A short hop with the regular walk animation playing through it; holding ↑ repeats the hops. At a walkable spot the same key enters the interaction instead. `player-jump.mp3`. `jumpKey` / `jumpDurationMs` / `jumpHeightPx` are config fields |
| Debug run (hold Shift) | done | Holding either Shift moves Rudy **4× faster** while walking — a testing aid (`DEBUG_SPEED_MULTIPLIER` in `src/domain/actors/player.js`) |
| Universal combat system (Rudy = thugs = the boss) | done | `src/domain/combat/combat.js`: one strike resolution for everybody; they differ only in their stats, speeds and move sets. The chronological arbitration lives in `src/domain/combat/arena.js` and is **pure** — it returns the events (`hit` / `miss` / `draw` / `ranged`), the app layer turns them into sounds and blood |
| Taking damage / HP / defeat | done | HP is a character parameter (Rudy 5, `lysy-blokers` 5, `zul-mietek` 12), 1 damage per blow, 0 = defeat. The defeated plays the **fall animation** (`deathRow` + `deathFrames` — 4 by default, **Rudy uses all 8** on row 2) while the body **falls the way the killing blow pushed it** — hit from behind, the enemy falls forward (owner 2026-10-24) — and the corpse persists across section visits. Killed mid-jump, Rudy's fall starts at once but the body finishes the hop and lands first. The end screen waits for the fall to finish before freezing the scene |
| Hit knockback | done | A landed melee blow pushes the target **100 px** away (`HIT_KNOCKBACK_PX` in `src/domain/combat/combat.js`), clipped flush at the walls. Ranged hits, misses and draws do not push. The displaced position of a lethal hit persists with the corpse |
| Blood VFX | done | Every landed blow sprays **3×3 px red squares** (`src/render/blood.js`) out of the struck character (player and enemies alike); they fly away from the blow, arc down and land on the struck character's feet line, staining the ground until the section is left. An attacker can switch bleeding off (`"bloodOnTarget": false`) — `zul-mietek` never makes the player bleed, but the player still bleeds him |
| Corpses that stay where they fell | done | The defeated lie where they fell and **keep lying there across section visits** (the kill is recorded in the session, keyed by the hostile's identity). A body also stays on the street when its `presentWhen` no longer holds — the boss keeps his corpse after his stage is over |
| Hostiles keep their place across section visits | done | Leaving a section and coming back finds the **living** enemies exactly where the player left them — the position, the facing and the patrol direction, not back at their post. The **HP is not persisted**: a wounded hostile comes back at full HP |
| Patrol enemy (`lysy-blokers`) | done | Paces **the whole section** — edge to edge of the screen, wherever he spawns; the `facing` of the street config decides which way he sets off (owner 2026-10-24). The **fight trigger comes from the street config** — `always`, `distance` (peaceful while the player is farther away than `distance`, 300 px here) or `hit` (peaceful until he is punched, then he fights back). In reach he **strikes immediately, with no reaction pause**; the only wait is between two headbutts (a 0.5 s hold at full stretch, then a 0.7 s recovery) |
| Boss (`zul-mietek`, the bum) | done | He exists only while his `presentWhen` holds (`{ "mission": [1, 2] }`) — before that he waits in the background as the `01-narutowicza-2-1.png` pose instead of the sprite. He is a **sentry**: he stands still, does not turn and cannot be hit until the fight starts. Then the **boss music loops** and the **introduction sentence plays once on top of it**. **One combined attack**: at the end of his animation a burst of green squares spits out — it hurts within ~300 px of the mouth, the squares land ~600 px away, and **jumping over the spray dodges it**. His fall moves the mission on to "Wypij piwo" |
| Duplicates look different (hue shift) | done | The **first hostile of a type keeps the original colors**; every further copy of that type in a section takes one of the random hues of the game, so the player can tell them apart. The hues are at least **45° away** from the original colors and from each other (`src/world/entity-hues.js`). The shift is **baked into the sheet's pixels when it is loaded**, so drawing costs exactly as much as before, and the **HP row of the hostile in the HUD carries the same shift** |
| Section transitions between the sections of a street | done | The sections of a street JSON form a chain (left to right); the player walks into the neighbouring section and back. The ends of the street are walled by convention (first section a left wall, last one a right wall) — there is no route, no `exitLeft`/`exitRight` and no `boundaries` in the config |
| Walkable interaction (the shop) | done | A spot of the section (`x1`…`x2`); ↑ walks Rudy in, he disappears for the trigger's `stay` and walks back out. The sound (`assets/sounds/<street>-<interaction>-<index>.mp3`) plays when he disappears in the spot, not when the walk-in starts. The trigger list holds one behaviour per world state (the **first** trigger whose `when` holds wins; an entry without a condition is the fallback and belongs last). The **exchange** — the mission effect, the item effect and the money effect — happens **300 ms before he leaves the spot** (`MISSION_LEAD_MS` in `src/app/walkable-interaction.js`), so the money icon and the beer icon swap in the same instant the new objective appears. Rudy **turns to the side he walks into** (owner 2026-10-22): a spot on the left half is entered facing right, one on the right half facing left |
| Main mission (spans the whole street) | done | The mission of a street is the `mission` field of its config — one log line per stage, the index is the stage. The stage moves on through the **trigger/effect model**: a trigger's `{"mission": 2}` effect sets the stage. A **defeated boss advances the stage by one** (its objective is done — "Pokonaj żula" → "Wypij piwo"), and a stage **past the last line** (a stage with no log line of its own) means **the end of the street** — that is how a config ends it |
| Mission logs | done | **Above the game area** (owner 2026-10-21): the log lives in the `#missionLogs` element of the page, right-aligned to the canvas' right edge — it is **not** drawn on the canvas any more. It shows the **objective that is still open** — the line of the current stage ("Kup piwo" → "Znajdź ustronne miejsce aby wypić piwo" → "Pokonaj żula" → "Wypij piwo"); a finished objective **disappears** when the next stage is reached. The logs come from the config only, survive section changes and are replaced with the next street's mission; a restart resets them |
| Mission-driven spawns (the mission decides what is in the world) | done | The mission stage of the config gates the boss (`presentWhen: { "mission": [1, 2] }`), the section overlays (`when: { "mission": [0] }`) and the walkable spots (`when: { "mission": [3] }` — the "wschodnia" spot is not a spot at all on the other stages, ↑ jumps as usual) |
| Section bosses | done | The `bosses` list of a section. A boss exists in the world only while his `presentWhen` condition holds, and his `fight` trigger (`always` / `distance` / `hit`) starts the fight. A boss that is not present any more does not block the street |
| Section overlays in two layers (`back` / `front`) | done | An overlay carries a `position` — `"back"` (the default) is drawn with the background, `"front"` is drawn **over the player and the enemies** (the street light of the third section stands in the foreground: whoever is behind it is covered by it). An overlay without a `when` condition is drawn **always** |
| Audio by convention | done | `<who>-attack` / `<who>-attack-hit` / `<who>-attack-miss` on every resolved blow, `<who>-jump`, `<street>-<section>-ambient` with the **`ambience.mp3` default street ambience** as the fallback, `<street>-boss-music` with the **`<street>-boss-intro` introduction sentence played once on top of it**, `<street>-<interaction>-<index>` for the interactions (the index of the trigger), and the end music **once, no loop**: `game-over` on defeat, `<street>-congratulations` on victory. Every file is optional (a missing one means silence). The ambience starts **automatically at boot** (plain play → muted start + unmute → a first-gesture retry, owner 2026-10-15) |
| Boss music over the ambience | done | The fight music **replaces** the street ambience for the duration of the fight, and the ambience comes back when the boss falls (owner 2026-10-21) |
| HUD (HP, money, items) | done | Top-left: hearts per HP, coins per money unit, then one icon per carried item unit; empty icon rows collapse — buying the beer swaps the coin row for the beer row in a single instant. The counters come from the **session** (`src/domain/quest/session.js`): the stats, the purse and the inventory. Top-right: **one row of hearts per hostile in fight mode, stacked one below the other** (several enemies at once no longer overlap). The **hearts of a row carry the hue shift of their hostile**, so the row belongs to the hostile of that color on the street |
| Pixel font | done | The bundled `PressStart2P-Regular.ttf` supplies Press Start 2P for all page and canvas text; the boot waits for the font to load. The end-screen headings, statistics, restart label and hint use fitted sizes |
| End screen (lost / won) | done | The game freezes and a **2/3-screen panel** appears over the scene with a big result message (**"PRZEGRAŁEŚ!"** / **"GRATULACJE! WYGRAŁEŚ GRĘ!"** — "Ukończyłeś wszystkie ulice!" after the last street), the statistics (**game time, defeated enemies, HP and money left**) and the **ZAGRAJ PONOWNIE** button (click or **Enter** — Space does NOT restart, owner 2026-10-15) → a fresh game (`session.resetForNewGame`, back to the first street, the hostiles at their posts). The end music plays **once, no loop**, then the panel stays silent. **No saves — session-only play** |
| Data-driven content (configurable via files) | done | The street JSON drives the whole street — mission, sections, `enemies`, `bosses`, `interactions`, `overlays`; the assets are picked by naming convention. A new street = a new file in `data/streets/` (the game finds the streets by name and plays them one after another, and ends after the last one) |
| Content schema v2 with validation | done | `src/domain/content/street-schema.js` and `character-schema.js` normalize and validate the configs into the **Street** and **Character** models. The old v1 shape (`trigger`, `highlight`, `give`/`cost`, the `mission` transition maps, the single `boss` object) is **not** supported: an unknown key is refused with a Polish error naming the file and the JSON path — no silent misreads. A character type without a config is not an error (it is skipped at spawn) |
| Unified trigger model (conditions + effects) | done | One concept everywhere: `{ "when": <condition>, "stay": <ms>, "effects": [...] }` — the sections (`onEnter`), the interactions and (later) the entities. The **first** trigger whose condition holds wins; a trigger without a condition is the fallback. Conditions: `mission`, `flags`, `items`, `money`, `defeated`, `position`. Effects: `{"mission": 2}`, `{"item": "beer", "count": 1}`, `{"money": -1}`, `{"flag": "x", "value": true}`. Implemented in `src/domain/quest/triggers.js` |
| Session aggregate (the state that survives a section change) | done | `src/domain/quest/session.js`: the mission, the inventory, the purse, the world flags, the character statistics, the play timer and the state of the hostiles (where they stand, where they fell). There are **no saves** — the session lives for one play-through (owner decision 2026-10-11); a restart rebuilds it from the player character config, and the **state of the hostiles is wiped with it**, so a new game starts with them at their posts (owner 2026-10-21) |
| One class for every hostile (the `Enemy`) | done | The old `Enemy` and the old `Boss` are **one** config-driven class (`src/domain/actors/enemy.js`): the role (enemy / boss) comes from the list the entity is in, the `behavior` (`patrol` / `sentry`) decides the AI and the `fight` trigger decides when the fight starts. A character with a `vomit` move is a **ranged** character (`attackKind` is derived) — it spits instead of trading blows |

## World data

| Street | Sections | Mission | Content |
|---|---|---|---|
| `01-narutowicza` | 1 "Zielona 3", 2 "Narutowicza 6", 3 "Narutowicza 14" | "Kup piwo" → "Znajdź ustronne miejsce aby wypić piwo" → "Pokonaj żula" → "Wypij piwo" | Section 1 = two `lysy-blokers` (patrol, they fight within 300 px) + the walkable shop "sklep" (x 400–500; the trigger moves the mission on, hands out the beer and takes the coin) + the shop sign overlay. Section 2 = the bum `zul-mietek` (a **sentry**: he waits in the background on stage 0, the fight starts on stage 1/2; **his fall moves the mission on to "Wypij piwo"**). Section 3 = the **street light** and the **metal barrier** in the foreground + the walking old man **`dziadek-biurowy`** (a **neutral patrol** — 1 HP, the `hit` trigger: he paces the section end to end, never starts a fight and dies to the first punch) + the walkable spot **"wschodnia"** (x 1200–1300, interactive on stage 3 only; it sets the mission past the last line → the end of the game — and takes the beer away) |

The streets are played one after another, in file-name order — the game finds them in `data/streets/` (the directory listing, or the manifest `streets.json`) and ends after the last one. A new street = a new JSON file (no code change).
